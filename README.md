# My Favorite Movie: *American Sniper*

*American Sniper* is an intense and emotional war film based on the life of Navy SEAL sniper Chris Kyle. It’s a gripping story of patriotism, sacrifice, and the psychological toll of war.

## Main Characters:
- **Chris Kyle**: The highly decorated Navy SEAL sniper, portrayed by Bradley Cooper. His story is one of heroism and personal struggle.
- **Taya Renae Kyle**: Chris Kyle’s wife, who stands by him through the challenges of military life.
- **Marc Lee**: A fellow Navy SEAL and friend to Chris, who plays an important role in the story.
- **Kyle's Military Team**: Fellow SEALs and soldiers who support Chris during his tours of duty.

## Why I Love It:
*American Sniper* is a powerful portrayal of war and the toll it takes on the men and women who fight. It’s not just a war movie, but a reflection on the emotional and mental strain that comes with serving in combat. Bradley Cooper’s performance is haunting, and the film shows the duality of heroism and personal conflict. It’s a film that stays with you long after it’s over.
